FP&A Analytics

What each sales channel actually costs

A cost model built on roughly 11,000 rows of synthetic outbound logistics billing data. It answers which sales channels actually make money once every cost of serving them is counted, not just the obvious ones. It allocates costs out to each channel rather than leaving them in one blended pile, keeps the channels unblended to avoid Simpson's paradox, separates the costs that are fixed by how the business is set up from the ones a manager can change month to month, tracks performance against targets, projects spend forward against a prior-year baseline, and flags data that looks wrong before it reaches the output. Review also surfaced a real error: a cost ratio had been computed as 18 months of cost over 12 months of revenue, overstating it. The corrected annualised figure is used throughout.
